# Learn Dashboard

> [!abstract]
> Choose a subject area. Each guide gives a recommended order and connects theory to configuration, labs, viva, and troubleshooting.

## Learning Areas

- [[Fundamentals Guide]] - Build the mental model needed for every later subject.
- [[Addressing and Subnetting Guide]] - Learn to design and validate IPv4 networks before configuring devices.
- [[Switching and VLANs Guide]] - Move from Ethernet switching through VLANs, trunks, loop prevention, link aggregation, and inter-VLAN routing.
- [[Routing Guide]] - Learn route selection from static routes through RIP, OSPF, and EIGRP.
- [[Network Services Guide]] - Configure the services that give clients addresses, names, time, files, web access, and email.
- [[High Availability Guide]] - Protect default gateways and understand first-hop redundancy choices.
- [[Security Guide]] - Secure device access, control traffic, translate addresses, and protect the access layer.
- [[Monitoring and Management Guide]] - Collect time, state, events, neighbors, and packet evidence for network operations.
- [[WAN VPN and Wireless Guide]] - Connect remote networks and users across serial, tunnel, encrypted, and wireless links.
- [[IPv6 Guide]] - Learn IPv6 addressing, neighbor discovery, automatic configuration, routing, and troubleshooting.
- [[Troubleshooting Guide]] - Use a repeatable evidence-based method and targeted symptom guides.
